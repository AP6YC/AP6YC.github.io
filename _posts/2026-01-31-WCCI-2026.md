I had three papers accepted to IJCNN/WCCI 2026 hosted in Maastricht in The Netherlands.
I was a primary author on the first paper outlining the `BinaryFuzzyART` algorithm along with the paper's first author Niklas Melton.
We supervised some undergraduate researchers in the writing of a hardware implementation (BFAMod) of the algorithm on a hybrid architecture chip (FPGA-CPU-GPU Zynq board).
Lastly, I mentored another student studying the robustness of `FuzzyARTMAP` against a series of adversarial learning scenarios.
Links to the paper submission are below:

- [BinaryFuzzyART][binaryfuzzyart]
